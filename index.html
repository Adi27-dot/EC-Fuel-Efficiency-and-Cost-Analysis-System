Core Mathematical Formulas

  • Fuel Required (per trip): Distance ÷ Mileage
  • Total Fuel Cost: (Fuel Required per trip × Fuel Price) × Number of Trips
  • Cost per km (CPK): Fuel Price ÷ Mileage
  • Estimated Chemical Energy: Fuel Consumed × Calorific Value
  • Estimated Budget Distance: (Budget ÷ Fuel Price) × Mileage (Note: Assumes constant mileage and price)

Definitions & Chemistry Scope

Fuel Efficiency (Mileage)

The distance a vehicle can travel per unit of fuel (e.g., km/L or km/kg). Higher mileage means less fuel is required for a trip, reducing overall costs.

Calorific Value

The amount of heat energy released when a unit of fuel is completely burned (MJ/L or MJ/kg). Values used are approximate reference figures.

Cost Per Kilometer (CPK)

The estimated fuel cost of driving 1 km, calculated as Fuel Price / Mileage. It is useful for comparing fuel expenses across vehicles, but excludes maintenance, servicing, depreciation and other ownership costs.

CO₂ Emission Factor

The mass of CO₂ released per unit of fuel consumed, expressed as kg/L for liquid fuels or kg/kg for mass-based fuels (CNG). Note: E20 and mixture values are estimates based on blend assumptions.
